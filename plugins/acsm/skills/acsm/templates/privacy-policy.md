## Notes for the drafter — delete before publishing

- **Hebrew is required** for Israeli consumers. Sections 1 through 6 and section 11 carry the
  substantive disclosures and need translating. ACSM does not translate them.
- Section 4 says you do not sell personal information. If you use advertising pixels, that
  statement is probably false under CCPA's definition of sharing, and the do-not-sell notice
  template applies instead.
- Section 3 needs a real retention period. "As long as necessary" alone does not satisfy
  California Civil Code section 1798.100 subsection a.
- If you process special category data — health, biometrics, religion, sexual orientation —
  this template is not sufficient. That needs counsel.
- The rights list must match what you can actually do. A stated deletion right with no working
  deletion endpoint is a representation you cannot meet.
