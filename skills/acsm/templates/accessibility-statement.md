## Standard we work to

We aim to meet **WCAG 2.2 Level AA**, published by the World Wide Web Consortium. It is the
standard referenced by the Americans with Disabilities Act guidance in the United States, by
EN 301 549 under the European Accessibility Act, and by Israeli Standard 5568.

## How we are doing

We currently meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA **partially**. That means most of the service conforms, and
some parts do not yet.

### Known problems

- Some data tables do not have proper header associations, so screen readers announce cells
  without their column names. We expect to fix this by a stated date.
- Two older PDF documents in our help centre are not tagged for screen readers. We will replace
  them with accessible web pages.
- Video tutorials published before a stated date do not have captions. We are adding them.

An honest list of gaps is worth more than a claim of full conformance. A statement claiming
full conformance while the service fails contrast checks is a representation you can be held
to, and it is easy to disprove.

## How we test

- Automated checks on every release, which catch roughly a third of issues.
- Manual keyboard-only testing of the main flows.
- Screen reader testing with at least one of NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver.
- Contrast checked against the design tokens, and against real rendered screens.

Automated tools alone do not establish conformance. Most criteria need a person.
