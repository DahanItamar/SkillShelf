## Notes for the drafter — delete before publishing

- **Hebrew is required** for Israeli consumers.
- **Do not add a class action waiver or mandatory arbitration clause from a US template
  without counsel.** They are unenforceable against consumers in the EU, UK and Israel, and
  including one can invalidate surrounding provisions rather than just failing on its own.
- Section 9's liability cap is drafted conservatively. A cap lower than fees paid is commonly
  struck down against consumers.
- If you serve businesses only, several consumer carve-outs above can be tightened. If you serve
  both, keep them — the consumer version has to survive.
- Section 11 cannot override the consumer's home forum in the EU or Israel. Do not let a
  boilerplate exclusive jurisdiction clause replace it.
